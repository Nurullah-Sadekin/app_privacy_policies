Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 8, 2026

Welcome to Video Converter! This privacy policy explains how our application handles your data. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

🔒 Our Privacy Commitment

We do NOT collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data or videos. All video conversion happens locally on your device.

1. Information We Don't Collect

Video Converter is designed with privacy in mind. We do NOT collect:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
  • Video files or any media content you convert
  • File names, paths, or metadata
  • Device identifiers or location data
  • Usage analytics or tracking data
  • Crash reports or diagnostic information

2. How the App Works

Video Converter operates entirely on your device:

  • Local Processing: All video conversions are performed locally on your device
  • No Cloud Upload: Your videos never leave your device
  • No Internet Required: The app works completely offline (after installation)
  • Your Files, Your Control: You choose the input and output locations

3. Permissions We Request

The app requires certain permissions to function, but only uses them for their intended purpose:

Storage Access

Why we need it: To read video files you want to convert and save the converted output.
How we use it: Only to access files you explicitly select through the file picker.

Photo Library Access (iOS)

Why we need it: To allow you to select videos from your photo library.
How we use it: Only when you choose to pick a video from your library.

4. Data Storage

The app may temporarily store configuration preferences (like last selected format or resolution) locally on your device for convenience. This data:

  • Stays only on your device
  • Is not linked to your identity
  • Can be cleared by uninstalling the app

5. Third-Party Services

Video Converter does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The app uses the following open-source libraries for functionality:

  • Flutter: UI framework (Google)
  • video_compress: Video compression library
  • file_picker: File selection dialog
  • path_provider: System directory access
  • permission_handler: Permission management

These libraries operate locally and do not collect or transmit your data.

6. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. Since we don't collect any personal data at all, the app is safe for all ages.

7. Security

Since all processing happens on your device and we don't transmit data, your videos and information are as secure as your device. We recommend:

  • Using device passcode/biometric protection
  • Downloading the app only from official app stores
  • Keeping your device OS updated

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
